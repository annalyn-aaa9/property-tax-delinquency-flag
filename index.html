Suggested Review Workflow
  1. Open the county Tax Collector portal for the client's property county.
  2. Search by property address, APN (Assessor's Parcel Number), assessment number, or fee parcel number — the search method varies by county portal.
  3. Review the current tax bill, outstanding balance, payment history, and delinquency status.
  4. Save the tax bill or search result in the client's Canopy file.
Note: Supplemental bills and prior-year delinquent taxes may be listed separately from the regular annual bill. Verify any outstanding balance with the county before finalizing the review.
